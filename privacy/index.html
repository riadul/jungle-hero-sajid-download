Jungle Hero Sajid: Privacy Policy

Publisher: Abacus IT, Adabor, Dhaka, Bangladesh (abacusitbd.com, info@abacusitbd.com). Last updated: 14 October 2026.

What the game collects

Nothing. Jungle Hero Sajid does not collect, send or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress, settings and the high-score table (including the name you type after finishing the game) on your own computer or phone. These files never leave your device. Uninstalling the game and deleting its folder (or clearing the site data, if you play in the browser) removes them.

Internet

The game is played offline. If you play the Steam version, Steam itself may record your achievements and playtime under Valve's own privacy policy.

Children

The game is made for children and families. Because it collects no personal information, no information about children is collected.

Contact

Questions: info@abacusitbd.com

This website

The game's home page (riadul.github.io/jungle-hero-sajid-download) sets no cookies and has no analytics or ads. Its fonts and pictures are served from the same site. The how-to-play video comes from YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) and only loads when you press its play button; YouTube's own privacy policy then applies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.
